Sternchen – Planning Phase Research Links

Research links that informed the planning of the offline-first, CVI-safe learning app for a child with PIGT-related cerebellar underdevelopment (MCAHS3). Note: This is a reassembled list from a fresh research pass, organized by planning topic. It mirrors the categories covered in the original plan.

1. The underlying condition: PIGT / MCAHS3

Multiple Congenital Anomalies–Hypotonia–Seizures Syndrome 3 — the genetic basis (PIGT / GPI-anchoring defect), cerebellar atrophy, seizures, and CVI association.

  • OMIM #615398 – MCAHS3 (PIGT) gene / syndrome
    Authoritative Mendelian genetics entry; gene, inheritance, phenotypes.
  • Spectrum of MCAHS3 (systematic review, 41 cases) clinical review
    Full phenotypic spectrum; cerebellar atrophy, intractable seizures, cortical visual impairment, developmental regression.
  • GARD – MCAHS3 about-the-disease page overview
    Plain-language overview including PIGT-CDG synonyms (LFSS, MCAHS type 3).
  • ClinGen – MCAHS3 condition curation classification
    Definitive gene-disease validity (PIGT, autosomal recessive, CDG GCEP panel).
  • Novel PIGT variant expands MCAHS3 phenotype (Genes 2016) case study
    Cerebellar degeneration + CVI as core features; neural defects as central.
  • Homozygous PIGT mutation leads to MCAHS3 (2018) case study
    Progressive cerebellar atrophy, refractory seizures, feeding difficulty.
  • NORD – MCAHS3 disease overview overview
    National Organization for Rare Disorders summary.

2. Cerebellar cognitive affective syndrome (CCAS / Schmahmann)

The cognitive-emotional-behavioural deficits caused by cerebellar underdevelopment — the target of the app's academic/functional levels.

  • The Cerebellar Cognitive Affective/Schmahmann Syndrome: a Task Force Paper key review
    Definitive overview: executive, visuospatial, language, emotion-affect deficits.
  • CCAS in cerebellar disease – bedside scale (Brain 2017) assessment
    Defines diagnostic features; working memory, fluency, visuospatial, attention.
  • CCAS/Schmahmann syndrome Task Force paper (open PDF) key review
    Dysmetria of thought; pediatric cerebellar mutism and behavioural changes.
  • CCAS – a Meta-analysis (The Cerebellum 2019) evidence
    Confirms deficits in language, executive function, visuospatial domains.
  • CCAS in a 12-year-old with acute post-infectious cerebellar ataxia pediatric case
    Pediatric criteria for CCAS: executive, visuospatial, personality, language.
  • CCAS in Ataxia-Telangiectasia – children pediatric study
    Age-graded cognitive deficits in children; spatial → executive with age.

3. Cortical Visual Impairment (CVI) – learning apps & frameworks

CVI-safe design principles (color preference, low clutter, single-focus targets) and comparable child learning apps that shaped the CVI-safe UX.

  • VisionLearn – Kotlin Multiplatform CVI learning app reference app
    Closest analogue: Perkins CVI framework, color preference, audio + TTS, cause-effect/matching levels.
  • CViConnect – CVI Range & Phases I–III framework
    Roman-Lantzy phases; single high-contrast target, color, movement, complexity.
  • Vizzbu – app for children with CVI (IIT Bombay) reference app
    Fixation, tracking, saccades; preferred colors; watch-then-play progression.
  • Bubbly – CVI Literacy App reference app
    Word "bubbling" for letters; colors, sizes, gestures, audio feedback, Switch Control.
  • Dynamic Difficulty Adjustment in Serious Games for CVI (2023) research
    Adaptive difficulty (reinforcement learning) for CVI visuoperceptual training.
  • Vision Nanny – AI platform for CVI platform
    Phase-specific visual therapy, contrast/color tools, therapist dashboard.

4. Design for ataxia / motor & cognitive disability (CP)

Inclusive design evidence behind the app's large targets, debounce for ataxia, reduced motion, latency, and offline-first approach.

  • Learning Software Application Design Model for Children with Cerebral Palsy design model
    Requirements → design model for CP learning software; motivational game design.
  • Usability Barriers & Inclusive Design in Mobile Learning Apps for Special Needs design study
    WCAG 2.2, large touch targets, sensory settings, predictable navigation.
  • Visual Perceptual Skills with AT-Supported App for Children with CP (2024) research
    Switch/eye-gaze support, gamification, levels of difficulty, RCT evidence.
  • RESNA 2025 – Switch-accessible tablet game design design case
    Cause-and-effect learning, switch access, haptic/visual feedback, customization.
  • Digital Accessibility for People with Cerebral Palsy guidance
    Avoid flashing/strobe (seizure safety), switch/adaptive input, AAC.
  • Digital Accessibility for Cerebral Palsy (W3C WCAG detail) guidance
    Flashing threshold, color-as-primary-indicator, iOS Switch Control.

5. Switch access (SWITCH_SCAN mode)

Basis for the switch-scanning input mode, acceptance/scan timing, and filtering of involuntary (ataxic) presses.

  • Switch Access to Technology – A Comprehensive Guide reference
    Scanning methods, highlight/feedback, acceptance delay, filtering involuntary presses.
  • Seven Stages of Switch Development (child-centred framework) progression
    Stage 1 single-switch cause-and-effect → step scanning with feedback.
